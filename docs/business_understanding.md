# Business Understanding

## Project
Executive Business Intelligence Analysis of Olist's Marketplace Performance

## Business Context
Olist is a Brazilian marketplace connecting small sellers to major online sales
channels. Olist manages logistics on behalf of sellers, meaning delivery
performance is directly tied to Olist's own reputation and customer
satisfaction — not just the individual seller's.

## Objective
Evaluate the overall health and performance of Olist's marketplace, and
identify the biggest opportunities for improvement — built as a
decision-support analysis, not just a data exploration exercise.

## Analytical Framework
Every business pillar is analyzed using a consistent three-question structure:

1. **What happened?** — the observed metric/trend
2. **Why did it happen?** — the driver behind the number
3. **What should management do?** — the recommended action

## Business Pillars
1. **Revenue & Growth**
2. **Operations & Delivery**
3. **Customer Experience**
4. **Seller Performance**

## Executive Summary (front page of the dashboard)
Revenue · Orders · Customers · Avg Review Score · Avg Delivery Time ·
Late Delivery % · Top Category · Top State · Key Insight · Key Recommendation

## Deliverable
- One Executive Summary page
- Four pillar drill-down pages
- A final Executive Recommendations section

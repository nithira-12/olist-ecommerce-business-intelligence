# Business Questions by Pillar

## 1. Revenue & Growth
- What happened? Total revenue, revenue trend over time, revenue by category, revenue by state
- Why did it happen? Which categories/regions are driving growth vs. decline? Is growth broad-based or concentrated?
- What should management do? Where to invest further, where to investigate underperformance

## 2. Operations & Delivery
- What happened? Average delivery time, % of late deliveries, delivery trend over time, delivery performance by region
- Why did it happen? Are delays concentrated in specific states/sellers? Any seasonal pattern?
- What should management do? Where to focus logistics improvement

## 3. Customer Experience
- What happened? Average review score, review score trend, score distribution
- Why did it happen? Does review score correlate with delivery delay, category, or price?
- What should management do? Prioritize delivery fixes vs. product/category fixes based on what actually drives satisfaction

## 4. Seller Performance
- What happened? Revenue by seller, revenue concentration among top sellers, seller-level late delivery rate and review scores
- Why did it happen? Is poor performance isolated to a few sellers or widespread? Is there revenue concentration risk?
- What should management do? Support programs for underperformers, flag concentration risk

## Known Data Gap
No marketing spend / customer acquisition data exists in this dataset, so
"why revenue grew" explanations are limited to category/region/seller mix,
not acquisition channel attribution.
